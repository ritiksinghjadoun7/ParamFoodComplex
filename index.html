Our story

Pure veg, because we eat here too.

Param started with a simple idea: put every kind of vegetarian food Gwalior loves under one roof, and cook it the way you would at home. No shortcuts, no compromise on what goes in the pan.

The family behind Param is vegetarian by choice, not by convention. That is why the kitchen runs on one standard rather than two, from the pav bhaji on the tawa to the cakes in the bakery counter.

Today that one roof covers a restaurant, a bakery, a sweet counter and a catering kitchen, across four addresses in the city.
